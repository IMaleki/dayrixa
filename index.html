The vision

AI utility with a product-first approach.

DAYRIXA explores how AI services and Web3 infrastructure can work together where programmable access, transparent allocation and interoperable digital credits provide a real functional benefit. The current phase is technical experimentation, not commercialization.

DAYRIXA AI Hub · Public Prototype

A research-assistant prototype is now part of DAYRIXA.

The DAYRIXA AI Hub currently includes an experimental research workflow for academic-source discovery, relevance ranking, publication intelligence, evidence synthesis, claim extraction, claim validation and claim-type classification. The interface supports English and Persian. Research outputs remain abstract- and metadata-level unless a source is reviewed in full.

01

Research Assistant

Discover academic sources and organize abstract-level evidence around a research question.

02

Claim Intelligence

Separate empirical findings, mechanisms, study objectives, background context and insufficient evidence.

03

Evidence Workspace

Rank sources, synthesize retrieved evidence and build a claim-to-source table with explicit limitations.
