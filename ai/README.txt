DAYRIXA AI Hub Prototype v13.1 — English + Persian UI
=====================================================
Adds an EN / فارسی language switch to the stable v13 checkpoint.
Persian mode switches the interface to RTL and translates core navigation,
research controls, evidence-table labels, validation labels, and project-status
language. Academic source titles, abstracts, claims, DOI metadata, and retrieved
research content remain in their original source language to avoid altering
evidence.
